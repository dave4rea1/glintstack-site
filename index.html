About

Hello, and welcome.

Glintstack exists because we kept running into small, everyday frustrations that nobody had bothered to solve properly — at least not for the people who actually live with them. So we started building the fixes ourselves.

We work end to end: the quiet plumbing underneath — storage, sync, encryption, the integrations no one sees — and the app that ends up in your hand. Whether it's for two people or ten thousand, it gets the same care.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What is Glintstack?
Glintstack (Pty) Ltd is an independent software company. We build mobile apps and the systems behind them, focused on everyday problems that deserve a careful, privacy-first solution.
Is Pockard free?
Yes. Pockard is free, with no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.
Do I need an account to use Pockard?
No. Pockard works fully offline without an account, and your cards stay on your phone. Signing in is optional and only needed if you want cloud backup or to sync between devices.
How does Pockard handle my data?
Card numbers and notes are encrypted on your device, including before any backup is uploaded, so we can't read them. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The full details are in the Pockard privacy policy.
How do I delete my Pockard account?
In the app, go to Settings → My data → Delete all my data. If you no longer have the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page.

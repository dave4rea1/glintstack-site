Hello, and welcome.

Glintstack exists because we kept running into small, everyday frustrations that nobody had bothered to solve properly — at least not for people here. So we started building the fixes ourselves.

We work end to end: the quiet plumbing underneath — storage, sync, encryption, the integrations no one sees — and the app that ends up in your hand. Whether it's for two people or ten thousand, it gets the same care.

We'd rather build fewer things and get them right. Each layer has to hold its weight before the next one goes on top. That's slower, but it's the only way we know how to make something you can actually trust.

Thanks for stopping by. If something here resonates — or annoys you — we'd genuinely like to hear about it.

— The Glintstack teamCape Town

What we believe

Three rules every layer has to pass.

i.

Local-first.

Everything we make is built for the people using it here — their shops, their data bundles, their phones — not adapted from somewhere else after the fact.

Lekker, not generic.
ii.

Privacy by design.

Data protection isn't a checkbox added at the end. It's in the architecture from day one: collect less, encrypt what matters, and make it easy to leave.

Your data is yours. Full stop.
iii.

Unhurried, on purpose.

We'd rather ship something right than ship it fast. No dark patterns, no growth hacks — just software that does its job and gets out of your way.

Slow is smooth, smooth is fast.

How we make things

Layer by layer, from the ground up.

Our name is the method. Every product goes up the same way.

first,

Start with a real problem.

Ideally one we've had ourselves. If we wouldn't use it every week, we don't build it.

then,

Pour the foundations.

Security, storage and privacy go in before a single pixel. They're the hardest to fix later, so we don't leave them for later.

next,

Make it feel good.

Fast, simple, kind to your battery and your data. The part you touch should feel like it was made by people who care — because it was.

always,

Keep listening.

Launch isn't the end. We read every email, and the next layer is shaped by the people using the last one.
